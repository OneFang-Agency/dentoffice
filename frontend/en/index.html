Services

We offer a wide range of dental services, from check-ups to implants.

Call to book

Everyday care

  • Oral exam

    A thorough check-up and a clear treatment plan.

  • Teeth cleaning

    Removing plaque and tartar, then polishing.

  • Cavity filling

    Tooth-coloured fillings that blend in with your smile.

  • Gum care

    Treatment for bleeding, sensitive or receding gums.

Restoration and surgery

  • Painless root canal therapy

    Gentle treatment under local anaesthesia to save the tooth.

  • Crowns and veneers

    Restoring the shape and colour of damaged or worn teeth.

  • Implants

    A secure, long-lasting replacement for missing teeth.

  • Oral surgery

    Extractions, including wisdom teeth, done carefully and safely.

Orthodontics and whitening

  • Orthodontics

    Braces and clear aligners for children and adults.

  • Whitening

    Professional whitening that is safe for your enamel.

Emergencies
